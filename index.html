About The Game

Forge Your Legacy

NEXUS: CONFLICT is a free-to-play tactical shooter that combines precise aiming mechanics with deep strategic gameplay. Choose from a diverse roster of Operatives, each with unique abilities, and compete in intense 5v5 matches across a variety of game modes.

Whether you're climbing the competitive ranks or just want to enjoy a quick match with friends, NEXUS: CONFLICT delivers an experience that rewards teamwork, communication, and skill.
